## Rules
- One pick per section. Two picks make a mess.
- Copy the pattern, never the pixels, the words, the logo or the images.
- Write the credit line before you build. Keep it as a comment in your code.
- Build in this order: navbar, hero, CTA, footer, 404.
- Check each section at 390px wide before you start the next.

## After the five
- Do the sections still look like one page? If not, fix the colours and fonts first. Then the spacing.
- Keep a list of every gallery you used. Credit them in your video notes or your README.
