# Brand inspiration list: real brand books, logos and DESIGN.md files
Every link was opened on 5 Oct 2026. Brand books belong to their owners. Study the structure. Do not copy a brand you do not own.

## Brand book checklist
Open a brand book. Tick each item you can copy into your own DESIGN.md. If the book skips it, write TODO.
- [ ] Logo: versions for light and dark, icon-only version, clear space, minimum size.
- [ ] Colour: hex codes, the job of each colour, and which one is the only accent.
- [ ] Type: font names, weights, sizes, line height, upper or lower case. Add a free substitute if the font is paid.
- [ ] Imagery: photo style, crops, illustration rules, icon style.
- [ ] Layout: grid, spacing scale, corner radius, shadow or border.
- [ ] Voice: three tone words and two real example lines.
- [ ] Misuse page: every "do not" the book shows. These become your Don'ts.
- [ ] Motion: speed and easing, if the book has a motion page.
Score: 6 ticks or more makes a strong source. Fewer than 4, find another book.
