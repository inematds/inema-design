# Five slide rules
1. Use the real logo of every tool you name.
2. Every slide has one idea and one moving graphic. No bullet lists.
3. Headline: 8 words at most. The line under it: 10 words at most.
4. Use real design sources, such as a design system from a real brand, and name them.
5. Make it look finished: real texture, no sparkle icons, no clipped text.

Before you build, show me three different styles. Wait for my pick.
