## How to write it
Write 5 to 7 mechanisms. A mechanism is a rule a critic can check by looking at a render.
- Useless: "Feels premium."
- Useful: "The headline is 5x the body size. There are three type sizes in total."
Write only what a person can see. Never write about the code.

## Mechanisms
1. Type: [sizes, weights, how many styles]
2. Colour: [how many colours, where the accent goes, how often]
3. Layout: [grid, alignment, what sits where]
4. Space: [how much air, where]
5. Motion: [what moves, how long, which direction]
6. Detail: [one small thing the reference does that most people miss]
7. (Optional) [anything else a critic can check by looking]

Examples to copy the style of:
- The headline is 5x the body size, with three type sizes in total.
- One accent colour, used at most twice per screen.
- Motion always resolves in one direction.
- Nothing animates for under 400ms.
- Whitespace above the fold is at least 40% of the frame.

## Rules for the critics
- Be harsh. Praise is not useful.
- Judge the render, never the code.
- Give a pass or a fail. Never a score.
- The craft critic puts ours next to the reference with labels stripped, says which is better, and names the single biggest gap.
- All three critics must pass: brief, system and craft.
- If a critic cannot see (no design-system.md, no render), say so. Do not carry on quietly.
