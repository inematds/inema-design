# Camera words Claude understands
Say these in your brief. Add a number when you can.

- Push in: the camera moves closer. Try "push in 8% over 3 seconds".
- Pull back: the camera moves away to show more.
- Slow every zoom to 0.7x: all zooms run at 70% of their speed.
- Hard cut on the beat: switch shots on a drum hit, with no fade.
- Whip pan: a fast sideways blur that hides a cut.
- Match cut: the next shot starts with the same shape in the same place.
- Rack focus: the sharp part moves from one thing to another.
- Parallax: layers move at different speeds, so the scene feels deep.
- Hold: stay still on the last frame for 1.5 seconds.
- Ease out: start fast, finish slow. It looks natural.
